* ---------------- LINIOWY WOBULATOR NAPIĘCIA ----------------
* leak=0: out(t)=limit(limit(IC,0,VL)+(VL/T)*t,0,VL).
* VL>0, T>0. T is the full-scale ramp time from zero, not from arbitrary IC.

.subckt WOBLIN out GDN PARAMS: VL=10 T=1 IC=0 leak=0 Taw=1m
* LLM_DESC_START
* Function: Linear wobulator (ramp 0..VL).
* Equation: see INTV; drive=VL/T. Linear only when leak=0.
* Pins: out, GDN
* Params: VL – machine voltage scale,
*         T  – ramp time from 0 to VL,
*         IC – initial output at t = 0,
*         leak - state decay rate [1/s]; Taw - anti-windup tracking time [s].
* LLM_DESC_END

* Wejście integratora – stałe napięcie VL/T
B_WOB_U u GDN V = { VL/T }

* Integrator z ograniczeniem do przedziału [0, VL]
* Wymaga zdefiniowanego w APM-1 subukładu INTV.
X_WOB_I out u GDN INTV PARAMS: IC={IC} SIGN=+1 L=0 H={VL} leak={leak} Taw={Taw}

.ends WOBLIN
